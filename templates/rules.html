Key Rules

  • Mercy Rule: 7+ goal lead after 2nd period = running time for 3rd period (even if gap drops below 7). 2-min and 5-min penalties still timed.
  • Timeouts: One 30-second timeout per game (regulation or overtime)
  • Jerseys: Coin toss determines home/away. Visitor wears white, home wears dark.
  • Roster: Max 20 players (18 + 1 or 2 goaltenders)
  • Minimum to play: 10 players in uniform + 1 goaltender
  • Bench staff: Max 5 accredited people behind the bench, max 6 total accredited per team
  • One team only: A player cannot play for two different teams
  • Injured players: Cannot sit on the team's bench
  • Gift exchange: Only the 6 starters (including goaltender) return to ice

Tiebreaker Rules

Article 9.7 - Hockey Quebec Administrative Regulations

The objective is to identify the best team among tied teams.

aHighest number of points
bHighest number of wins
cLeast goals against
dMost goals for
eQuickest goal scored in all games played
fMost Franc Jeu (Fair Play) points
gBy a draw

Note: If a team does not show up for a game, all games played against that team by other teams shall not be considered.

Overtime Rules

Article 9.6.1 - Hockey Quebec Administrative Regulations

Elimination Games (1/8 round, Quarter-Finals)

  • Single 5-minute overtime period, stop time
  • 3v3 plus goaltender (except penalized players)
  • First goal scored ends the game
  • Penalty = add 1 player to the non-penalized team
  • If still tied after 5 minutes: shootout

Semi-Finals & Finals

  • Single 10-minute overtime period, stop time
  • 3v3 plus goaltender (except penalized players)
  • First goal scored ends the game
  • Penalty = add 1 player to the non-penalized team
  • If still tied after 10 minutes: shootout

Shootout Rules

Article 9.6.2 - Hockey Quebec Administrative Regulations

  • Home team chooses to shoot 1st or 2nd
  • Round 1: Each coach designates 3 players to shoot
  • Shots taken in alternating turns (no simultaneous shots)
  • If still tied after Round 1, coaches designate players one at a time
  • All players (except goaltender) must shoot before anyone goes again
  • Players serving penalties at end of overtime may participate
  • Penalty shot playing rules are in effect
  • Team with most goals in a completed round wins

Protests

Article 7.4 - Hockey Quebec Administrative Regulations

  • No protest on an official's judgment call (referee, linesman) will be considered
  • Step 1: Notice of protest must be given to the referee at time of infraction or next stoppage. Must be recorded on the scoresheet.
  • Step 2: Follow up in writing within 1 hour after the game to the registrar, accompanied by a $200 cash deposit or bank transfer.
  • Deposit refunded only if the claimant receives a favourable decision
  • Tournament discipline committee decisions are irrevocable and cannot be appealed

Ineligible Members

Article 2.4 - Hockey Quebec Administrative Regulations

  • Submit complaint in writing to the arena registrar within 1 hour after the game
  • Must include proof of ineligibility and a $200 cash deposit
  • Deposit refunded only if the claimant receives a favourable decision
  • The committee's decision is binding

Additional Rules

  • Any player suspended in their league must have served the suspension before playing in the tournament. Suspensions cannot be served during exhibition games.
  • All players automatically grant the tournament rights to use their image for publicity, photos, social media, etc.
  • No mascots or flag bearers on the ice or players' bench
  • Teams shake hands at end of games (officials may cancel if incident occurs)
  • A medical clinic will be set up in each arena
  • Use of tournament logo without permission is strictly forbidden
